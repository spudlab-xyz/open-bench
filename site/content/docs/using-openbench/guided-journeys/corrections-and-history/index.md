## Investigate a correction without losing the original

1. Open the reading in **Data** and inspect specimen, run, point, acquisition, and source.
2. Change the value or quality only after checking the original evidence.
3. Enter a precise revision note.
4. Open its **vN** chip or **Data audit** to inspect old and new values.
5. Open global **History**, search for the measurement, and compare versions.
6. Refresh affected analyses and conclusions; obtain a new report review for the new data revision.
