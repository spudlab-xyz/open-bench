Use these guides when you need to complete a specific task rather than look up an individual field.
They complement the [guided research journey](../guided-journeys/) and [field reference](../field-reference/).

## Choose a task

- [Resolve readiness issues](resolve-readiness/) — translate every automated warning into a safe next action.
- [Import measurements from CSV](import-csv/) — map columns, inspect rejected rows, and preserve provenance.
- [Build the scientific context](scientific-context/) — add sources, instruments, calibration, and evidence.
- [Manage projects, conditions, and specimens](projects-and-specimens/) — make administrative and design changes safely.
- [Save and reuse a procedure](reuse-methods/) — copy procedure content without copying validation claims.
- [Write conclusions and manage attachments](conclusions-and-attachments/) — preserve interpretation and local evidence.
- [Exchange experiments and restore archives](exchange-and-restore/) — choose the right transfer and recover from validation failures.
- [Browser and accessibility guidance](browser-and-accessibility/) — understand storage, keyboard behavior, printing, and compatibility.
